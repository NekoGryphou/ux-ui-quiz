# Kanom’UI

**Good design is easy to recognize. Until you have to explain why.**

A button that disappears when you need it. A form that seems determined to make you give up. Two perfectly reasonable-looking interfaces, except one is quietly making everyone's life harder.

Welcome to **Kanom’UI**, a UX/UI quiz where the interfaces have problems and you're the one who has to find them.

Made for curious minds at **Kanoma** who like asking, _“Wait, why is it like that?”_

## So, what's the game?

You'll be dropped into realistic (and sometimes suspiciously familiar) interfaces. Take a look, trust your instincts, and figure out what could be better.

Sometimes the mistake jumps right out. Sometimes it takes a second look. Sometimes you realize you've made the exact same design decision before. No judgment. Well, maybe a little.

There are **111 questions** to explore, with a **30-question selection** in a full run. Each one is a chance to sharpen your eye, challenge your assumptions, and pick up a thing or two about making interfaces nicer to use.

## Four ways to question everything

- **Pick your answer.** One choice, one design decision. How confident are you?
- **Find the culprits.** Sometimes there's more than one problem hiding in plain sight.
- **Point at the problem.** Click directly on the part of the interface that's causing trouble.
- **Choose your fighter.** Two versions enter. One makes more sense. Pick the better design.

Expect a mix of usability, accessibility, interaction patterns, visual hierarchy, and those tiny details that make the difference between _“this feels right”_ and _“why won't this work?”_

## Play, learn, repeat

A full run gives you 30 randomly selected questions, so you won't necessarily face the same challenges twice. When you're done, you can look back at your answers and understand what you spotted, what you missed, and why it matters.

Not quite ready for another full round?

**Quick 10** is there for a shorter brain workout, and **Retry the ones I missed** lets you revisit the questions that got the better of you.

Your progress and scores are saved in your browser, so you can come back for another go without starting your story from scratch.

## Not just a guessing game

The goal isn't to memorize a list of UX commandments or declare every unusual design choice a crime against usability.

It's to get better at noticing problems, thinking about the people using an interface, and explaining _why_ one choice works better than another.

Behind every question is a practical lesson. And behind every questionable interface is a reminder that even small decisions can have a big impact.

**See something weird? Good. You're learning to look closer.**

## Want to run it locally?

You'll need Node.js and pnpm. Then:

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm dev
```

Open the local address shown in your terminal and start spotting problems.

## A little note

Kanom’UI was made for **internal learning at Kanoma**. The fictional interfaces are intentionally imperfect: they're here to be questioned, not copied into production.

Some included images have usage rights that would need reviewing before a public release.
